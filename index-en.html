Before sending feedback

  1. Note your device model, operating system version, app version or TestFlight build number.
  2. Explain what you did, what you expected and what happened. Use fictional data to reproduce the issue where possible.
  3. Include only redacted errors. Do not send API keys, app passwords, invitations, complete receipts, ledger databases or identity documents.
  4. For sync issues, say whether photo sync was enabled, whether multiple devices were involved and which step failed. Do not send server login details.

During testing you can also submit feedback through TestFlight. We will not ask for passwords or complete financial data to provide help. This mailbox does not provide transfers, investment advice or professional accounting or tax advice.

Data control and recovery

You retain your lawful rights in your data and decide whether to save, export, delete or share it; other people's lawful rights remain unaffected. Protect devices, service credentials and original photos, and regularly verify independent backups. JSON/CSV exports do not include photos, and sync is not an independent historical backup.

There is currently no developer-hosted official ledger or recovery service. We generally do not hold your local data or decryption credentials and cannot promise to recover lost data. See the user agreement for risks and statutory responsibilities; your rights in data do not exclude the developer's responsibilities under law.

For privacy requests, use the subject “YiKuaiJi privacy request” and describe the access, correction, deletion or withdrawal you need. We verify only the minimum information necessary and handle requests as required by applicable law. We explain available options and limitations for copies held by independent providers or other members.

Current version and purchases

The app is currently in testing and release preparation, not formally released. The planned first release is a one-time paid App Store download in China mainland and Hong Kong, not an auto-renewing subscription. Third-party storage and model fees are separate. Final regions, prices and included features are those shown on the actual store page. This site has no checkout or payment links.

The agreement and privacy notice remain release-preparation drafts. Publishing them does not mean filing, legal review or App Store review is complete. Refunds and other consumer rights follow store procedures and applicable law; a one-time purchase does not categorically exclude them.
