YiKuaiJi · Documentation and support

YiKuaiJi has a built-in expense Agent that prepares transaction evidence for your review. The iOS app is free to download, core bookkeeping works offline, and JSON/CSV exports need no subscription. Stay in control of your own and shared records. This site provides instructions, privacy information and contact details. It does not offer online ledgers, account sign-in, payments or file uploads.

Everyday life, recorded together

From version 0.1.1, the first-launch quick start introduces basic entries, transaction evidence and advanced features. Skip it or reopen it anytime in Settings → Quick start. Settings → User guide contains the complete offline instructions.

Capture any proof of a transaction, not just receipts, including invoices, payment screenshots and transfer confirmations. Check the amount, date and currency after recognition. For advanced sync or AI assistance, first read the WebDAV and external model setup chapters.

Built-in expense Agent: a workflow connecting on-device text recognition, field structuring and validation to prepare the merchant, amount, date and currency. You review and confirm every entry. Apple's on-device model is available on compatible devices and systems; external model APIs are configured by you and receive text only after confirmation. The Agent prepares drafts and never silently records transactions. Model capabilities depend on your device, system and chosen service.

Choose to use it without paying: manual entries, on-device text recognition, category statistics and export require no subscription or purchased AI credits. WebDAV and external AI are optional. Third-party providers set their own fees and limits; unlimited cloud AI usage is not included.

Take your records with you: export JSON/CSV, merge imported entries or filter and export selected records for spreadsheets, migration and analysis. Exports exclude photos, drafts and service keys and are not a complete app backup. Keep original attachments separately.

Shared sync: keep a ledger with family, your partner or travel companions. Configure your own WebDAV service to sync encrypted entries. Photo sync is off by default and can be enabled per ledger. Multiple ledgers, category filters and statistics work offline without enabling sync.

Before sending feedback

  1. Note your device model, operating system version, app version or TestFlight build number.
  2. Explain what you did, what you expected and what happened. Use fictional data to reproduce the issue where possible.
  3. Include only redacted errors. Do not send API keys, app passwords, invitations, complete receipts, ledger databases or identity documents.
  4. For sync issues, say whether photo sync was enabled, whether multiple devices were involved and which step failed. Do not send server login details.

If you use a TestFlight build, you can also submit feedback through TestFlight. We will not ask for passwords or complete financial data to provide help. This mailbox does not provide transfers, investment advice or professional accounting or tax advice.

Data control and recovery

You retain your lawful rights in your data and decide whether to save, export, delete or share it; other people's lawful rights remain unaffected. Protect devices, service credentials and original photos, and regularly verify independent backups. JSON/CSV exports do not include photos, and sync is not an independent historical backup.

There is currently no developer-hosted official ledger or recovery service. We generally do not hold your local data or decryption credentials and cannot promise to recover lost data. See the user agreement for risks and statutory responsibilities; your rights in data do not exclude the developer's responsibilities under law.

For privacy requests, use the subject “YiKuaiJi privacy request” and describe the access, correction, deletion or withdrawal you need. We verify only the minimum information necessary and handle requests as required by applicable law. We explain available options and limitations for copies held by independent providers or other members.
