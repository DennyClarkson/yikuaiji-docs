1. Who processes data and the app's limits

The current version requires no YiKuaiJi account and has no operating official cloud ledger or subscription service. The device stores local data. WebDAV and external model services you actively choose process their respective requests.

The operator is 薛正尧. Support and privacy requests go to yikuaiji_feedback@163.com. Using the app does not transfer rights in your entries or photos to us. You retain lawful control and choices, while others' existing rights remain unaffected. “Local only,” “bring your own service” and “your data” cannot replace the operator's statutory duties to protect, inform and assist.

2. Information stored on the device

The device stores ledger names, base currencies, categories, amounts, merchants, dates, notes, author/member identifiers, sync versions, drafts, recognized text, photo attachments and preferences. These support display, editing, statistics, export and the sync or processing you choose.

API keys, WebDAV credentials, shared keys and presets use system secure storage. Ordinary ledger databases, attachments and exports should not be treated as an equally protected, additionally encrypted vault. Protect device unlocking and avoid sensitive content on shared devices. System backups or chosen file locations may create additional copies.

The current iOS version enables system file sharing for app documents. Its Documents directory, including the internal database and attachments, may be visible in Files or computer file sharing. Do not share the whole app directory or directly modify/delete internal files externally. Storage and export arrangements will be reassessed before formal release.

The client has no advertising tracking or developer-operated behavioral analytics. Stores, operating systems and test distribution may process purchase, installation or crash information under their own settings. This is not a claim of “zero data collection” in every environment.

3. Camera, photos, files and local recognition

The camera is used for photos or scanning. Image selection and imports/exports use system pickers or file access, with permissions varying by platform. Content is used for the feature selected. You can decline and continue manual bookkeeping, and revoke permissions in system settings.

iOS/macOS use system Vision; Windows uses on-device OCR. Android source uses ML Kit, whose packaging and initialization must be checked before that platform ships. Available Apple system models provide on-device processing. Installing the app does not enable sending photos to external models by default.

Images may contain EXIF or other capture metadata. The app does not currently promise to strip all metadata. Check sensitive photos before import or sharing, especially for unrelated people, identity documents or financial account details.

4. WebDAV: encrypted content, recipients and visible information

Shared operations are transmitted to your chosen WebDAV address only after configuring sharing and running manual/quick sync, or actively enabling automatic sync conditions. Encrypted content may include ledgers, categories, entries, member information and deletions. Drafts are not confirmed sync records.

Photo sync is off by default for each device and ledger. When enabled, associated receipt/item photos are transmitted encrypted with the shared key; missing remote photos can be skipped and retried later. Entries and photos use AES-256-GCM. The storage provider can still see authentication, IP addresses, request times, directory identifiers, file sizes and other network/storage information.

Members with the shared key and directory access can read decrypted content. Ordinary invitations contain the key; quick invitations also contain an encrypted address, username and password. Copying puts content on the system clipboard. QR codes can be photographed and some systems sync clipboard content. Protect invitations as credentials and clear the clipboard when appropriate.

Storage location, server logs, version history and retention depend on your chosen provider and account settings, not one operator designated by the app. Check safeguards before choosing a service and avoid giving members access to your entire personal drive.

5. External models: what text is sent and to whom

Before each single or batch request, the app displays the currently configured domain and model and asks for confirmation. It sends OCR text blocks and IDs, barcode/QR candidates, processing prompts and model parameters, not original images, device file paths or text coordinates. Content you put in custom prompts is also sent.

Your API key is sent in authentication headers to the configured service. The service also receives IP addresses, request times, model usage and other network information. Fetching models and testing the connection request only the model list and do not send real receipts, but still include authentication.

For example, the official DeepSeek endpoint sends requests to api.deepseek.com. With a custom domain, the recipient is that domain's service. Selecting the DeepSeek preset does not by itself guarantee the endpoint is official. The example is not a partnership or guarantee.

Recognized text may include names, contact details, spending locations, account information or other sensitive content. Sending “text only” is not anonymity. Check the service's privacy policy, processing regions, retention, training uses and deletion channels. If unclear, use local features and do not send sensitive information. The app cannot guarantee that every user-configured service has been reviewed.

6. Other connections, exports and diagnostics

Automatic exchange rates access the European Central Bank's public rate endpoint. Default automatic mode may update while the app is in the foreground. It does not send entry amounts, photos or member lists, but the source receives normal network request information. Switch rate settings to manual mode to stop automatic fetching.

JSON/CSV exports create readable files at your selected location without additional encryption. They do not package photos or the complete app configuration. A cloud-drive destination may automatically upload exports independently of the WebDAV photo switch.

Developer mode is off by default. If enabled, recent external-model diagnostic sessions are held in app memory and may include recognized text, prompts, responses and errors. Turning it off clears those sessions. They are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Remove sensitive content before copying diagnostics or submitting feedback; hiding keys alone does not anonymize spending information.

If you email support, the operator receives your email address, message and voluntarily attached files to reply, troubleshoot and handle rights requests, not for advertising or training. The receiving mailbox uses NetEase 163. The email provider processes mail transmission, storage and related network information. Submit only necessary redacted information; do not send complete ledgers, receipts, keys, passwords or identity documents. Information is retained only as needed for the request and then deleted or anonymized. Where law requires retention, use is restricted and information is handled when that period ends. Provider backups and logs follow its separate policies.

Public documentation and support pages use GitHub Pages. The site has no accounts, payments, ledger upload forms, developer analytics or advertising tracking, and does not read app data. GitHub may still process IP addresses and access logs for security and service operation, so this is not “no data collection whatsoever.” GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Emailing or following third-party links enters those services.

7. Retention, deletion and withdrawing choices

Confirmed local records generally remain until you delete them or clear app data. Drafts remain until confirmed, deleted or cleared. The recycle bin currently retains items for 30 days; expired items may be permanently deleted when cleanup runs, not necessarily erased from every device and server immediately on day 30.

Permanent deletion of an individual shared entry attempts to remove associated remote historical operations and photos. A minimal deletion marker prevents offline devices from reviving old data. Connectivity and appropriate permissions are required. There is no one-click remote wipe of an entire shared ledger. Archiving, disconnecting, disabling photo sync and uninstalling are not remote deletion.

You can stop automatic/photo sync, disconnect WebDAV, remove presets or model configurations, revoke keys/app passwords at the provider and revoke system permissions. Stopping use does not retroactively undo completed processing; requests already started may still be completed or billed by the provider.

To remove an entire remote ledger, coordinate members, disconnect relevant devices and verify the exact directory. The folder owner should delete content and history at the provider and revoke access. Third-party logs, system backups, exports and member copies need separate handling. The developer cannot promise to erase every copy held by independent parties.

8. Cross-region processing and third-party safeguards

Local files are on the device and in user-selected backups. WebDAV and model data may be processed in providers' disclosed regions, which may differ from your or your device's location. HTTPS and content encryption do not guarantee that data stays within one jurisdiction.

Providing personal information to others, processing sensitive information or transferring it across borders requires assessing applicable law, identifying recipients, purposes, safeguards and rights channels, and obtaining separate consent or meeting other requirements where necessary. This general notice does not replace those requirements. Default/example services and the scope of configurable services need compliance verification before release; this draft does not guarantee every custom service is suitable.

9. Your choices, minors and contact

You can view, correct, export and delete local entries in the app and stop optional external processing as described above. There is no YiKuaiJi account-deletion flow because no official account service is active. Close third-party services with their respective providers.

For children's or others' information, first obtain necessary authorization and follow applicable protections. Do not use the app to collect or publish others' sensitive data without consent. If you cannot establish lawfulness, do not upload or share.

For help, contact 薛正尧 at yikuaiji_feedback@163.com with a subject such as “YiKuaiJi privacy request.” Describe the information you want to access, correct or delete, or your other request. The operator will respond within applicable legal time limits and, when necessary, request only minimum proportionate verification, never ledger decryption keys or account passwords. Where the operator cannot directly read or delete local, third-party or member copies it does not hold, it will explain its limits and provide workable guidance. This does not excuse refusing requests it must handle under law. You retain the right to complain to relevant regulators or seek other lawful remedies.

Material changes to data purposes or services should update this notice and obtain renewed consent when required. The formal effective date and regional legal review remain pending. This notice is not compliance certification or a blanket exemption from liability.
