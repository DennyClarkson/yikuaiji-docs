1. Service scope and relationship to other terms

YiKuaiJi is an offline-first everyday shared bookkeeping tool with manual entries, on-device recognition, drafts, statistics, imports and exports, and optional user-selected WebDAV sync and external text processing. It does not collect or transfer money on your behalf and is not a banking, payment, investment, professional accounting or tax service.

This document explains current features and their limits. When distributed through Apple, the applicable Apple standard end-user license agreement and store terms continue to apply according to their provisions. This document does not replace the store EULA or reduce mandatory local consumer rights. Reading these terms or using local bookkeeping is not consent to send data externally.

3. Local storage, drafts and backups

Ledgers, entries, drafts, photos and settings are mainly stored on your device. Normal exit from the editor attempts to preserve a draft, but power loss, storage failure, process termination or uninstalling can make data unrecoverable. Keep important original evidence and regularly verify independent backups.

Manage device unlocking and access, storage locations, shared members, service accounts, keys and invitations. Check backups before upgrading, changing devices, uninstalling, bulk deletion or force uploading. Safely retain original photos and necessary credentials separately and actually test import and recovery. Do not keep your only copy in a sync folder or rely solely on drafts.

There is currently no official ledger service hosted or continuously backed up by the operator. The operator generally does not hold your local data or decryption credentials and cannot promise recovery after device loss, file deletion, key loss or a provider outage. Purchasing the app does not purchase data custody, disaster recovery or guaranteed permanent availability. This does not waive the operator's responsibilities for software quality, security or other matters under law.

Current JSON/CSV exports are not complete app backups: they do not package photos or all drafts, keys and settings. System backups may separately retain app data, depending on the system and your settings. “Offline-first” must not be presented as a promise that data never leaves the device.

4. Recognition, recorders and exchange rates

OCR, on-device models and external models generate only content for confirmation. They may omit, misread or invent information. Check dates, currencies, amounts, merchants and categories before confirming. Do not treat results as professional advice or your sole accounting evidence.

The payer/recorder shown in the interface primarily comes from entry-author metadata. It is not identity verification, proof of actual payment or confirmation of debts between members. Statistics use saved amounts and exchange rates and are not bank reconciliation.

Automatic rates come from the European Central Bank's daily reference data. The app calculates cross-rates, modifying the source information. Reference rates do not represent actual transactions, fees or future movements. Source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html . Terms: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/disclaimer/html/index.en.html .

5. WebDAV sharing authorization and risks

After configuring sharing, you can preview and confirm manual sync, actively use quick sync or enable foreground automatic sync. Quick/automatic sync does not show a full checklist each time when there are no conflicts. Sync transfers encrypted operations, not the whole database. Photos are not uploaded by default; transfer requires photo sync to be enabled for the relevant ledger on that device.

Members with remote-folder access and the shared key can decrypt and copy shared content and may modify or delete it. Ordinary invitations contain the key. Quick invitations also contain the service address, username and password protected by a numeric passcode. They are not automatically expiring or individually revocable invitations. Share privately only with trusted members, preferably using independent credentials restricted to a dedicated folder.

There is currently no interface for per-member permissions, member removal or key rotation. Disconnecting in the app does not revoke server access. Encryption reduces the risk of unauthorized reading but is not an absolute security guarantee and cannot recall copies already downloaded by members.

6. Third-party model and storage services

External processing must be requested by you and confirmed with the target service and model before sending. Batch confirmation covers the selected batch. Requests include OCR text blocks and IDs, barcode/QR candidates, prompts and model parameters, but not original photos. Text can still be personal or sensitive information. The API key is sent to the service you configure as an authentication credential; it does not “never leave the device.”

Fetching model lists or testing a connection also contacts that service with credentials, but sends no real receipts. Storage and model services may log network information, retain requests or charge fees. Check their actual terms, processing regions and your account settings. Do not send sensitive information to services whose safeguards you cannot verify.

Provider names and examples explain compatible configuration only, not a partnership, endorsement guarantee or promise of permanent free access. Third parties bear their legal responsibilities, and the YiKuaiJi operator remains responsible for its own duties under law. User-configured services do not create a blanket exemption.

8. Deletion, withdrawal of choices and stopping use

You can edit/export entries, disable automatic or photo sync, disconnect WebDAV, remove model configurations and revoke permissions in system settings. Declining optional external processing does not prevent basic manual bookkeeping.

Deleted entries first enter the recycle bin. Items beyond the current 30-day retention period may be permanently removed during cleanup. Shared-entry deletion and restoration sync between devices. Permanently deleting an individual shared entry attempts to remove remote historical operations and associated photos, retaining a necessary minimal deletion marker to prevent old devices from re-uploading it. Completion depends on connectivity, permissions and other devices' state.

There is no one-click remote cleanup of an entire shared ledger. Disconnecting, archiving, disabling photo sync or uninstalling does not remove server content, third-party logs, system backups or member copies. The folder owner should verify the scope, coordinate members and perform cleanup in the storage service. Immediate erasure from all backups cannot be promised. See the guide and privacy notice for details.
